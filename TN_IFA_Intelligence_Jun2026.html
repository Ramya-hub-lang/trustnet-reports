June 2026 · IFA Research Sentiment

IFA research activity in June shows a defensive rotation bias, with Multi-Asset Income and Global Equity Income funds dominating the top 10 for the second consecutive month. Four new entrants this month — Orbis Global Balanced, Royal London Sustainable Leaders, Fidelity Special Situations, and Rathbone Global Opportunities — all entered the top 10 despite Q2–Q3 performance quartile rankings, pointing strongly to content and sentiment-driven demand rather than performance chasing. Notably, Artemis Global Income held the #1 position with a +24% MoM spike that coincides with a 3× increase in editorial coverage, providing the clearest content-drives-research signal in the dataset to date. Browser investigation this month identified that Orbis is running a homepage leaderboard ad campaign — solving a previously unexplained +38% spike. Royal London and Rathbone remain genuinely organic discoveries with no advertising or content, representing the highest-priority cold commercial calls. The risk-off environment (US tariff uncertainty, gilt yield pressure) is sustaining elevated interest in capital-preservation strategies, with the IA Mixed Investment 40–85% Shares sector up 31% MoM in IFA research volume.

What's Driving IFA Behaviour This Month
Four macro signals that explain the broader pattern in the data above.
📥
Inflow Signal
Net positive fund flow data (proxy from research volume × AUM) suggests IFAs are actively allocating, not just monitoring. Strongest in Multi-Asset and Global Equity Income.
🛡
Risk-Off Rotation
US tariff uncertainty and gilt yield volatility driving IFAs away from Europe and Fixed Income toward capital-preservation mandates. Second consecutive month of this pattern.
🔍
Stable Discovery
Four funds entered the top 10 for the first time with no performance change. This is discovery behaviour — IFAs exploring alternatives, likely driven by content, peer recommendation, or adviser events.
📊
Content Amplification
First month where a direct content→research correlation is measurable. Artemis citicode article volume tripled; factsheet views rose 24%. The content-to-factsheet feedback loop is real and now provable.
