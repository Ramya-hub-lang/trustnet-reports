Fund Objective

The trust aims to produce a greater return than the S&P 500 Index over a five-year rolling period, after deducting charges. The trust invests primarily in equities of companies listed in the United States. The trust may also invest in other transferable securities, money market instruments, warrants, cash and near cash and deposits. The trust is actively managed with no reference to a benchmark.

Fund Manager · Baillie Gifford · Managing since 2015
Tom Slater has managed Scottish Mortgage since 2015, alongside James Anderson until 2022. He joined Baillie Gifford in 2000 and has been integral to shaping the trust's philosophy of identifying transformational growth companies early. His focus spans listed and private companies globally, with particular expertise in technology, healthcare and emerging markets.
