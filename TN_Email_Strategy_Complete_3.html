⚠️  Trustnet's email programme is disconnected, unmanaged and incomplete. Users are force-opted into newsletters they didn't choose. Unsubscribes aren't respected. Emails carry legacy Financial Express branding from a domain that no longer represents the business. And the single biggest email opportunity in UK fund data — the Crown Rating change alert — has never been built. The result: a 10% open rate, a compliance risk and a missed commercial opportunity.

⭐ Recommendation — Option A

One system. Lower cost. Simpler preference centre. Perfect unsubscribe sync. The only challenge is Charlotte agreeing to move away from MailChimp — that's a conversation, not a blocker.

Option B is acceptable short term if Charlotte is not ready. But plan to migrate to Option A in Phase 3.
