TrustNet AI Summary
Scottish Mortgage is a high-conviction global growth trust managed by Tom Slater and Lawrence Burns at Baillie Gifford. It holds concentrated positions in disruptive technology and innovation companies, including private holdings that represent approximately 30% of the portfolio. The trust trades at a 12.9% discount to NAV — historically wide — which may represent an opportunity for long-term investors comfortable with higher volatility. Its 5 Crown Rating reflects strong risk-adjusted returns over a full market cycle, though recent years have been challenging due to the growth-to-value rotation. Best suited to IFAs seeking long-term capital growth for clients with a 10+ year horizon and high risk tolerance.

Scottish Mortgage remains one of the most distinctive investment trusts available to UK IFAs. Under Tom Slater's stewardship, the portfolio maintains its long-term orientation toward transformational companies — both listed and private. The current 12.9% discount to NAV is wider than the 5-year average, partly reflecting persistent uncertainty around private company valuations which represent approximately 30% of assets. The trust's 5 Crown Rating reflects genuinely superior risk-adjusted returns over the full market cycle, including the painful 2022 drawdown from which it has substantially recovered. For IFAs, the key consideration remains whether clients have the temperament to hold through periods of significant underperformance relative to index.
TrustNet View: Suitable for long-term growth portfolios with a 10+ year horizon. The wide discount presents a tactical opportunity for patient IFAs. Not appropriate for income-focused mandates or clients with low volatility tolerance.
