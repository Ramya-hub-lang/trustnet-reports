Top 5 Critical Gaps
Must fix in the next 60 days
Critical

1. No AEO-structured content pages — Crown Rating, Alpha Manager, SRRI have no FAQ schema. AI platforms cannot cite Trustnet for these foundational queries. This is the single biggest AI citation gap.

2. No email capture on high-intent pages — 308K factsheet views per month with zero email prompt. Every factsheet view is a warm lead walking out the door uncaptured.

3. No Compare CTA on factsheet pages — Clarity shows 19% of users immediately search for another fund after landing. No compare button = missed engagement = pages/session stuck at 1.72.

4. Schema programme partially live but 12 PBIs pending dev — NewsArticle, BreadcrumbList, Organization, Person and ItemList schemas are live. Sending these to dev this week unblocks the next phase of AI citation improvement.

5. Fundswire content not differentiated from editorial — Sponsored Fundswire articles look identical to editorial. No trust signal for readers. AI platforms do not cite sponsored content as authority.

Top 5 Biggest Opportunities
Highest revenue and growth potential
Strategic

1. Crown Rating data licensing — Trustnet's Crown and Alpha ratings are used informally by the industry. A formal data licensing model to platforms like HL, AJ Bell, and Morningstar creates a new B2B revenue stream with no additional content cost.

2. IFA audience activation — 10,899 IFA Signal users identified at F1 79% precision. This is a commercially valuable audience that fund managers would pay to reach. Fundswire is currently selling inventory to this audience at commodity rates — it should be sold at premium.

3. Fund comparison as a product — Users want to compare funds (Clarity data proves it). A structured Fund vs Fund comparison page format — 10 pages to start — creates a linkable, rankable, citable page type that currently does not exist on Trustnet.

4. AI-first content programme — With 510 AI referral sessions already arriving, a structured programme to improve citations from 12/24 to 18/24 would roughly triple AI-driven traffic by September 2026.

5. Newsletter / email programme — Trustnet has a Subscribe option but no structured CRM programme. An IFA-specific weekly email using Most Researched Funds data as the lead would generate a recurring premium touchpoint with the highest-value audience.

Editorial Strategy

Content gaps and opportunities

Trustnet has strong editorial talent — Gary Jackson, Jonathan Jones, Matteo Anelli, Emmy Hawker, Patrick Sanders — but the content structure is not optimised for AI citation, search visibility or audience retention.

The fundamental editorial gap

Trustnet's editorial team produces high-quality analysis but the content is structured for human reading, not for AI citation or structured search. Every article should have schema markup, internal links to fund factsheets, and Q&A sections that AI platforms can extract as answers. None of these are currently present.

Editorial · Gap
AEO Content Pages — Crown Rating, Alpha Manager, SRRI
These three concepts are core to Trustnet's identity but have no dedicated, AEO-structured pages. Each needs a standalone page with: a clear definition, 8–10 FAQ questions in schema format, methodology explanation, and links to top-rated funds. These pages would directly fix the AI citation gaps identified (SRRI at 0%, Alpha Manager partial, ISA queries lost to competitors).

Product & UX

Experience gaps holding engagement back

Clarity heatmap data (5,145 factsheet views, 1,742 real clicks) reveals exactly where users are frustrated. Pages/session sits at 1.72 against a 2.4 target. The product is not guiding users to their next action.

What Clarity data tells us

19% of all clicks on factsheet pages go directly to the search box — users are immediately searching for another fund. This is the single clearest UX signal in the data. Users want to compare but the product does not offer a visible comparison path. Add a Compare CTA next to the search box and pages/session will increase materially.

UX · Critical Gap
No Compare CTA on Factsheet Pages
Clarity confirms 19% of clicks go to fund search on factsheet pages. Users are manually searching for comparison funds because there is no Compare button visible. A "Compare with another fund" CTA next to the search bar — which pre-fills the current fund — would directly convert this intent into a comparison journey, increasing pages/session and fund_compare events.
Pages/session +0.3 fund_compare events Time on site
UX · Critical Gap
Register CTA Not Prominent Enough
The Engaged Retail audience has 17,441 users viewing 3+ factsheets but not registering. The Register CTA is in the header but not contextually surfaced at the moment of highest intent — when a user is deep in factsheet research. A mid-page Register prompt ("Save this fund to your portfolio — register free") at the point of third factsheet view would convert significantly better than the current header-only placement.

It also increases session depth as users compare the Stay vs Switch score across multiple funds.

Technical SEO & AI Visibility

The infrastructure of discoverability

Trustnet's technical SEO foundation is functional but not optimised. The schema implementation programme (12 PBIs pending dev, 5 already live) is the single highest-leverage technical project — it will directly improve both Google rich results and AI platform citations.

AI citation = the new SEO

Strong foundation already in place — NewsArticle, BreadcrumbList, Organization, Person and ItemList schemas are all live. 510 sessions per month are already arriving from AI platforms with a citation score of 12/24. At 18/24 (the September 2026 target) this would approximately double to ~1,000 sessions. At 22/24 — achievable with the full schema and AEO programme — AI referral could reach 2,000–3,000 sessions per month. AI referral is growing faster than any other channel and Trustnet is positioned to dominate fund research queries if it moves now.

Commercial Strategy

Revenue gaps and new streams

Trustnet's commercial model is primarily Fundswire (sponsored content) and data licensing. Both have significant untapped potential, and there are two entirely new revenue streams that the audience and product data now make possible.

The commercial gap in one sentence

Trustnet is selling commodity CPM advertising to a premium audience. 10,899 identified IFA users and 295 high-value commercial users represent an audience that should command 5–10x the CPM of a generic financial website. The data infrastructure to prove and sell this audience is now in place — it is not yet being used commercially.

Commercial · Opportunity · New Revenue
Premium IFA Audience Product
The IFA Signal audience (10,899 users, F1 79% precision) is now validated and measurable. Fund managers want to reach IFAs specifically — not general investors. Trustnet can now offer a "Reach 10,899 active IFA researchers" audience product at a premium CPM, backed by GA4 data and the D13 precision model. This reframes Fundswire from content sponsorship to targeted audience activation — a significantly higher-value proposition.
New revenue stream Premium CPM Fundswire differentiation
Commercial · Existing Revenue · Growth Opportunity
Crown Rating Licensing — Expand an Existing Revenue Stream
Crown Ratings are already published for all applicable funds. A licensing model already exists for fund groups wishing to use the rating on their own marketing materials or website. The opportunity is to grow this — actively approach fund managers who are using Crown Ratings in their materials without a formal licence, expand distribution to platforms and aggregators (HL, AJ Bell, Morningstar), and create a tiered licensing structure. Each new licensing deal also means Trustnet is cited on that platform, improving AI citation and brand visibility.
