Why Now

The investment management industry is restructuring around AI. The data below is from published research in 2026.

Wealth platforms cannot write fund research themselves
Banks and wealth platforms serve millions of investors who ask which funds to consider. They do not have research teams to answer that question. They either leave it unanswered — or they buy independent content. TrustNet is that content.
The way content is consumed is changing
Platforms are embedding AI chatbots. Analysts are querying AI tools for research. A static news feed on a web page is no longer sufficient. Clients need content that is AI-queryable — not just readable.
FE fundinfo is not in the AI content ecosystem yet
Claude already connects to FactSet, LSEG, Morningstar and others for financial data. No source for independent UK fund editorial exists in that ecosystem. TrustNet can be the first — and it is harder to displace a source once analysts build workflows around it.
30 years of content — no competitor can replicate this overnight
TrustNet has been writing independent UK fund research since 1996. That archive — 30 years of fund analysis, manager commentary, sector views — is a unique data asset. Citywire is a competitor. Morningstar does not write this content. Nobody else does.
Revenue is already proven — this is not a new market
Allfunds and Lloyds Bank are already paying for TrustNet editorial content today. They resell it to Barclays Private Wealth and Lloyds portal customers. The commercial model exists. MCP is a better version of what is already working.
