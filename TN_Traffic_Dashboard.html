Key Findings for Giles
What this data means for TrustNet

Why MCP is essential
AI Referral custom channel shows 9,044 tagged sessions. But the real AI traffic (chatgpt.com, claude.ai, perplexity.ai) is embedded in the 705K Referral and 650K Unassigned buckets — not yet broken out separately.
Referral and Unassigned together are 1.36M sessions — 14.4% of all traffic. Properly tagging AI sources within this is the immediate next action for Ramya.
Email has the highest engagement rate (81.43%) and longest session time (2m 18s) — TrustNet's most loyal users come via email alerts, not organic search.
MCP Phase 1 (News) will capture AI agent queries in structured logs for the first time — we'll know exactly what IFAs ask AI about TrustNet funds.
Next action: filter Referral traffic in GA4 by source to isolate chatgpt.com, claude.ai, perplexity.ai and add them to the AI Referral channel group.
