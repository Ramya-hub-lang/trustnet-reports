How users are identified and targeted

Step 1 — User registers on Trustnet. On registration, the Preference Centre modal (TN-D8-001) asks: Who are you? The answer sets user_type in Umbraco CRM (adviser / investor / other).

Step 2 — Behavioural signals refine targeting. GA4 tracks factsheet_view, track_fund, filter_type=IFA events. Users matching IFA Signal criteria are flagged in HubSpot via the GA4 → HubSpot integration.

Step 3 — HubSpot assigns to email stream. Based on user_type + behavioural signals, HubSpot automatically assigns each contact to the correct email stream. IFA advisers → IFA Briefing. Retail investors → Retail Weekly.

Step 4 — HubSpot handles retail broadcast. Retail Weekly is a higher-volume, lower-personalisation send — HubSpot handles this at scale.

Execution Plan

From zero subscribers to first send

Eight weeks from approval to first email send. We start with zero subscribers — the first 4 weeks are entirely about building the infrastructure to acquire and segment subscribers before a single email is sent.

8-Week Launch Plan

Pre-Launch — Approval
Get stakeholder sign-off
Present this strategy to manager and commercial team. Agree on: platform ownership (HubSpot vs HubSpot split), content ownership (Ram + Gary + Commercial), revenue targets, and 8-week launch timeline. No subscribers yet — nothing to send until approved. Owner: Ram.
Week 1 — Foundation
Audit + Platform Setup
Audit existing email platform — how many subscribers, what is currently sending, GDPR compliance status. Set up HubSpot contact properties: user_type, fund_interest, ifa_signal (boolean). Connect subscribe form to HubSpot. Owner: Ram + Dev.
Week 2 — Integration
Umbraco → HubSpot Sync
Build nightly sync: Umbraco user profiles → HubSpot contacts. Map user_type = adviser to IFA Briefing list. Map user_type = investor to HubSpot Retail Weekly list. Build GDPR unsubscribe webhook. Owner: Dev (TN-D8-001 Preference Centre required).
Week 3 — Templates
Build Email Templates
Build 4 email templates in HubSpot (IFA Briefing, Crown Alert, Fund in Focus) and HubSpot. All use Trustnet brand colours — teal #00A499, dark #253746. All links include GA4 UTM parameters. All pass email client testing (Litmus or Email on Acid). Owner: Ram + Design.
Week 4 — Content
First Edition Content Preparation
Pull Most Researched Funds data from GA4 for first IFA Briefing. Gary Jackson writes editorial pick. Commercial team confirms first sponsored slot. Crown Rating data feed connected to HubSpot for Crown Alert automation. Owner: Ram + Gary + Commercial.
Week 5 — Testing
End-to-End Testing
Send test emails to internal team across all clients (Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail, mobile). Verify UTM parameters fire correctly in GA4. Verify unsubscribe flow updates Umbraco. Check HubSpot automation triggers correctly for each segment. Owner: Ram + Dev.
Week 6 — Soft Launch
IFA Briefing — First Send
Send IFA Briefing to IFA Signal audience (10,899). Monitor open rate, CTR, unsubscribes in first 24 hours. Check GA4 for email channel sessions. Report results to stakeholders within 48 hours. Owner: Ram.
Week 7 — Retail Launch
Retail Weekly — First Send
Send Retail Weekly to Engaged Retail audience via HubSpot. Monitor results. Begin A/B testing subject lines. Owner: Ram.
Week 8 — Full Programme
All 4 Streams Live
Crown Alert automation live (fires on rating update day). Fund in Focus first send. Full programme operational. Revenue reporting begins. Owner: Ram + Commercial.
